TACO Manager

A Windows desktop tool that lets TikTok creators sign in with TikTok and view their own account statistics in one dashboard.

What it does

Sign in with TikTokUsers connect their own TikTok account through the official TikTok Login Kit. TACO Manager never sees or stores TikTok passwords.
Account statisticsShows follower count, likes count, video count and views of the user's public videos, using the TikTok Display API.
Runs locallyThe app runs on the user's own Windows computer. Data is stored on that computer only.
Disconnect anytimeUsers can disconnect their account in the app, or revoke access from TikTok's settings.

Permissions requested

  • user.info.basic — open ID, display name, avatar, to identify the connected account.
  • user.info.profile — username, to show which account is connected.
  • user.info.stats — follower, following, likes and video counts.
  • video.list — the user's public videos and their view counts, to calculate total views.

TACO Manager does not post, edit, delete, like, comment or follow on behalf of users.
