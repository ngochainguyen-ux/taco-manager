Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what data TACO Manager (“the App”) handles and how. The App is a Windows desktop application that runs on your own computer.

1. Data we access from TikTok

Only after you authorize the App with TikTok Login Kit, and only within the permissions you grant:

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name, avatar URL, username.
  • Statistics: follower count, following count, likes count, video count.
  • Your public videos’ IDs and view counts (used to calculate total views).

We never receive your TikTok password.

2. How the data is used

Solely to display your account statistics to you inside the App. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Where data is stored

  • All data is stored locally on your computer in the App’s database.
  • Access and refresh tokens are encrypted with Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI), tied to your Windows user account.
  • The developer operates no server that receives your TikTok data. The App communicates directly between your computer and TikTok’s official API.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your computer until you delete it.
  • Disconnecting an account in the App revokes the token with TikTok and deletes it locally.
  • Uninstalling the App and deleting its data folder removes all stored data.
  • You can also revoke access anytime in your TikTok account settings.

5. Children

The App is not intended for users under 18.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above shows the latest version.
